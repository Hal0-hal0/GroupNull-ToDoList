ASAP: Assign, Schedule, Act, and Prioritize

Welcome to ASAP: Assign, Schedule, Act, and Prioritize, Group Null's to-do list app designed to help students manage and organize their school tasks efficiently. Our built-in assistant, Gibinib, is here to guide you through navigating assignments, deadlines, and priorities.

Features

📝 Task Management by Subject

Organize tasks based on specific subjects.

Easily classify your work into categories like quizzes, reports, exams, or presentations.

📅 Deadline Tracking

Input deadlines for each task, and let the app calculate how many days are left.

Stay ahead by receiving notifications when tasks are overdue.

📊 Flexible Display Options

Choose how tasks are displayed:

Alphabetical order

Sorted by deadlines

Custom sorting algorithms

Default view

Select between ascending or descending order for clearer organization.

🤖 Assistant "Gibinib"

Your personal assistant for task management.

Gibinib helps you input, categorize, and navigate through your tasks effortlessly.

How It Works

Input Tasks: Add tasks by subject and specify their type (e.g., quiz, report, exam).

Set Deadlines: Enter due dates for your tasks, and the system will calculate remaining days.

Organize & Sort: Use our display methods to view tasks the way you prefer.

Stay Updated: Keep track of overdue and upcoming tasks with our organized views.

Display Options

Alphabetical: Sort tasks from A-Z or Z-A.

By Deadline: Show tasks closest to their deadlines first.

Sorting Algorithm: Choose a sorting method for personalized organization.

Default View: Display all tasks without filters.

Why Use ASAP?

🕒 Save Time: Let Gibinib handle the organization so you can focus on your studies.

🎯 Stay Focused: Prioritize what matters most with clear, structured views.

📚 Manage Workload: Avoid last-minute rushes with deadline tracking.
